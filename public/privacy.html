Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains what data Lucky Daily ("the App," "we," "us") collects and how it's used.

1. Information stored on your device

Your saved number combos, pull history, and theme preview settings are stored locally in your browser (using local storage) and never leave your device. We don't have access to this information, and it isn't transmitted to us.

2. Information collected for Premium subscriptions

If you subscribe to Premium, checkout is handled entirely by our payment processor, Paddle.com, acting as Merchant of Record. Paddle collects the billing information (such as email and payment details) needed to process your subscription and issue receipts. We receive confirmation that your subscription is active, but we do not see or store your payment details. Paddle's own privacy policy governs how they handle that data.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data. We don't use third-party advertising trackers in the App. We don't require an account or email address to use the free features.

4. Cookies and similar technology

The App may use local storage (not traditional cookies) to remember your saved numbers and app preferences on your own device, as described above.

5. Children's privacy

Lucky Daily is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@deacondigits.com.
