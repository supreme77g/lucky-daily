Refund Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Deacon Digits is free to use. Every feature — Today's Board, Daily, Numbers, and My Numbers — is available to everyone at no cost, so there's nothing to purchase and nothing to refund.

1. Optional tips

If you choose to leave a voluntary tip through Ko-fi, that tip is processed entirely by Ko-fi, not by us. Tips are a gift to support the project, not a purchase of any feature or content, so they're generally non-refundable. That said, if you tipped by mistake or have a billing concern, reach out to Ko-fi directly, or email us at support@deacondigits.com and we'll do what we can to help.

2. Contact

Questions? Email support@deacondigits.com.
