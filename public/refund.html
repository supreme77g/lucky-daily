Refund Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Lucky Daily Premium is a monthly subscription billed at $1.99/month through Paddle.com, our payment processor and Merchant of Record.

1. Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel your Premium subscription at any time. When you cancel, you'll keep Premium access through the end of the billing period you already paid for — we don't cut off access early, and we don't bill you again after that period ends.

2. Refund requests

Because Premium is billed monthly and can be cancelled anytime before the next charge, we generally don't offer refunds for partial months already used. That said, if you were charged in error, charged after cancelling, or have another billing issue, email us at support@deacondigits.com within 7 days of the charge and we'll review it.

3. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued by Paddle back to your original payment method and may take a few business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

4. Contact

Questions about billing or refunds? Email support@deacondigits.com.
